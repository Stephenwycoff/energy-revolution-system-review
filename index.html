04 — The claims

The three big claims, in plain English

  1. “Up to 7x the energy of a solar panel.” Extraordinary, and the sales page shows no independent measurement. Verify before building anything.
  2. “Cuts bills by 88% in 30 days.” Depends entirely on your home, climate, rates, and how much you implement. Assume nothing is typical.
  3. “Suppressed by the energy industry for 80 years.” A story device used to sell the product. It is not evidence the device works.
None of that means there’s nothing useful inside. It means buy this as an inexpensive set of plans and ideas — not a proven appliance.
